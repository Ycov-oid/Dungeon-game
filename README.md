A Console Dungeon Crawler

A text-based RPG for the Windows console, written in C. You are a victim of Amos’s endless wars, descending through five dungeon floors to claim the legendary Aether Core. Along the way you’ll fight monsters, gather materials, craft potions, and face bosses that test your brain as much as your blade.

Features
Five dungeon floors, each with three mob encounters followed by a boss
Puzzle bosses, where attacking means solving a problem under a time limit:
Geometry Gate – area of a rectangle
Physics Archer – distance, speed, and time
Bridge of Pythagoras – hypotenuse calculation
The Heavy Boulder – force, mass, and acceleration (F = ma)
M.A.D.Z – type a random signature before the timer runs out
Crafting system with 12 recipes: healing, strength, and stun potions, plus Return Potions, Monster Aroma, and Intelligence Potions
Loot and materials that drop per floor, plus gold and XP
Leveling system that raises your max HP and damage
Town hub with a Potion Store, Crafting Bench, Church (heal for gold), and Inventory
Save and load support (savegame.dat)
Monster Aroma to make a run harder (1.5x stats) for better rewards
Colorful animated UI: flashing title, loading bar, and typewriter-style backstory

How to Play
Start a New Game and enter your adventurer’s name.
Visit the Potion Store to stock up on healing potions.
Enter the Dungeon and pick a floor. Clear three mobs, then face the boss.
Collect materials, then use the Crafting Bench to make stronger potions.
Heal at the Church and Save before tackling harder floors.
Defeat M.A.D.Z on Floor 5 to reach the Aether Core.
Tip: Intelligence Potions reveal the answer to a boss puzzle, which is very useful on the later floors.
